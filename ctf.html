Mission: find the orange flag and return to the cyan base. Their locations, cover, sentries, and supplies vary between maps. Walls stop movement and shots. Health (green), ammo (gold), and energy (cyan) packs replenish after 600 frames. Carrying the flag slows you down.

24 actions: 8 compass directions × 3 abilities (move, fire, dash). Every AI action includes movement. Space or Shift without a direction uses your facing direction. Fire aims at the nearest enemy with a clear line of sight; with no visible enemy it fires along your movement direction. The same aiming rule applies to both controllers.

Limits: 100 health, 30 ammo, 100 energy. Shots cost 1 ammo with a 14-frame cooldown. Dash costs 20 energy with a 45-frame cooldown. A mission ends on delivery, destruction, or 3,600 frames (60 simulated seconds). Enemy patrols and firing are scripted opponents; the network receives no example actions.

Controller: human · firing uses shared automatic aim.

THE OBJECTIVE YOU TEACHEDITABLE

Every reward, in the open.

Discount factor γ = 0.99. Double DQN uses reward + γ × next Q value, except on delivery or destruction. The 3,600-frame time limit has no extra penalty and still bootstraps the next Q value. Hidden activations are tanh; outputs are linear Q values. Exploration decreases toward 3%; resumed runs begin at 20%, fresh runs at 100%. Training seed: 2026; evaluation seeds: 9000–9004. Reset weights uses the same seed for repeatable comparisons. Replay: 30,000 transitions, batches of 32; Huber loss and Adam (learning rate 0.0006).

Try setting objective progress to 0 to test sparse rewards, raising the damage penalty to encourage caution, or raising the flag and delivery rewards relative to combat. Use Reset weights for a fresh comparison. Reward values are incentives, not guarantees of behavior.

WHAT THE NETWORK CAN SEE96 VALUES

Sensors. State. Choices.

  • 16 robot/objective values: position, velocity, health, energy, ammo, shot/dash cooldowns, flag possession, offsets to flag and base, and facing direction.
  • 32 directional sensors: 8 compass sectors × ranges to walls, visible enemies, visible supplies, and visible hostile projectiles. Range is divided by 1,000; 1 means no detection. Wall rays use exact rectangle intersections, including thin maze walls.
  • 32 enemy values: 4 fixed enemy slots × relative position, velocity, health, range, visibility, and alive state. Hidden enemies expose only alive state; other values are zero.
  • 16 supply values: 4 known supply stations × relative position, type (0 health / 0.5 ammo / 1 energy), and availability. Supply locations and cooldown availability are known.

Coordinates/offsets scale by map width and height. Health/energy scale by 100, ammo by 30, robot velocity by 18, enemy velocity by 1.2, enemy health by 60. Cooldowns scale by their maximum. All inputs are clamped to [−1, 1]. No raw pixels or pathfinding solution is given to the network.
